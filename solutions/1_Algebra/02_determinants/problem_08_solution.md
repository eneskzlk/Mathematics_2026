# Exercise 8 — Determinants

The determinant is multiplicative: $\det(XY)=\det X\det Y$.

$$
\det(AB)=3(-5)=-15,\qquad
\det(BA)=(-5)(3)=-15.
$$

Since $\det A=3\ne0$, the inverse exists and has determinant $\det(A^{-1})=1/3$. Therefore

$$
\det(A^{-1}B)=\frac13(-5)=-\frac53.
$$

Although the actual matrices $AB$ and $BA$ need not be equal, their determinants are both the product of the same two scalar determinants.
