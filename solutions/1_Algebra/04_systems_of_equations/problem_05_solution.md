# Exercise 5 — Reading Row Echelon Form

The augmented matrix represents

$$x+2y-z=3,\qquad y+2z=1,\qquad 0=0.$$

There are two pivot variables ($x,y$) and three unknowns, so exactly one free variable. Choose $z=t$. Then $y=1-2t$ and

$$x=3-2(1-2t)+t=1+5t.$$

Thus the infinitely many solutions are $(1+5t,1-2t,t)$ for all $t\in\mathbb R$. Substitution confirms both equations.
