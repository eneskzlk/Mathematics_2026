# Exercise 6 — Solving with the Inverse

$$A^{-1}=\frac1{2\cdot3-1}\begin{pmatrix}3&-1\\-1&2\end{pmatrix}=\frac15\begin{pmatrix}3&-1\\-1&2\end{pmatrix}.$$

Hence

$$x=A^{-1}b=\frac15\begin{pmatrix}15-7\\-5+14\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}8/5\\9/5\end{pmatrix}.$$

Check: $2(8/5)+9/5=5$ and $8/5+3(9/5)=7$, so $Ax=b$.
