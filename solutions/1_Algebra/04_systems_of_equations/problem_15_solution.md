# Exercise 15 — Three Types of Systems

**System 1:** Subtract equation 2 from equation 1: $2y=2$, so $y=1$ and $x=1$. There is **exactly one solution**, $(1,1)$.

**System 2:** Subtract twice equation 1 from equation 2: $0=0$. The equations are equivalent, so there are **infinitely many solutions**, $(x,y)=(t,2-t)$ for $t\in\mathbb R$.

**System 3:** Subtract twice equation 1 from equation 2: $0=1$. This is a contradiction, so there is **no solution**.

The elimination distinguishes a pivot in each variable, a redundant row, and an inconsistent row, respectively.
