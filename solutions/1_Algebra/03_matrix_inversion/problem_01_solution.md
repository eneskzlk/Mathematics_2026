# Exercise 1 — Definition of the Inverse

With $A=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix}$ and $B=\begin{pmatrix}1&-1\\-1&2\end{pmatrix}$, direct multiplication gives

$$AB=\begin{pmatrix}2-1&-2+2\\1-1&-1+2\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix}.$$

Similarly,

$$BA=\begin{pmatrix}2-1&1-1\\-2+2&-1+2\end{pmatrix}=I_2.$$

Thus both inverse identities hold, so $B=A^{-1}$.
