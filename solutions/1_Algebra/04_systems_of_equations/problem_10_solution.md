# Exercise 10 — Polynomial Through Three Points

Write $p(x)=ax^2+bx+c$. Substitution of the points gives

$$c=1,\qquad a+b+c=3,\qquad4a+2b+c=9.$$

Thus $a+b=2$ and $4a+2b=8$, so $2a+b=4$. Subtracting yields $a=2$, hence $b=0$ and $c=1$.

Therefore

$$\boxed{p(x)=2x^2+1}.$$

Checks: $p(0)=1$, $p(1)=3$, and $p(2)=9$.
