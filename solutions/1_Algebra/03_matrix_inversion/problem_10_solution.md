# Exercise 10 — Diagonal Matrices

$D=\operatorname{diag}(2,-3,5)$ is invertible since every diagonal entry is nonzero:

$$D^{-1}=\operatorname{diag}(1/2,-1/3,1/5).$$

Checking diagonal products gives $DD^{-1}=\operatorname{diag}(1,1,1)=I_3$. Matrix $E=\operatorname{diag}(2,0,5)$ is singular: its middle row is zero and $\det E=0$, so no inverse exists.
