# Exercise 9 — Two Linear Conditions

## Question

Find numbers $x,y$ satisfying

$$
x+y=10,\qquad 2x+5y=32.
$$

Solve the system and explain geometrically why the solution is unique.

## Solution

Subtract twice $x+y=10$ from $2x+5y=32$:

$$
3y=32-20=12\quad\Rightarrow\quad y=4.
$$

Then $x=10-4=6$. Check: $6+4=10$ and $12+20=32$.

The lines $x+y=10$ and $2x+5y=32$ have different slopes ($-1$ and $-2/5$), so they intersect at one point only: $(6,4)$. The coefficient determinant is $1\cdot5-1\cdot2=3\ne0$.
