# Exercise 5 — Matrices and Basic Operations

Multiply each row of $A$ by the column $x$:

$$
Ax=\begin{pmatrix}
2(4)-1(2) \\
1(4)+3(2) \\
\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}
6 \\
10 \\
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The columns of $A$ are $a_1=(2,1)^T$ and $a_2=(-1,3)^T$. The same result is

$$
4a_1+2a_2=4\begin{pmatrix}
2 \\
1 \\
\end{pmatrix}
+2\begin{pmatrix}
-1 \\
3 \\
\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}
6 \\
10 \\
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Thus the components of $x$ are the coefficients of a linear combination of the columns.
