# Exercise 13 — Redundant Equation

From $x_1+x_2=10$ and $x_2+x_3=8$, subtract the second from the first:

$$x_1-x_3=10-8=2.$$

This is precisely the third equation, which adds **no new information**.

Set $x_2=t$; then $x_1=10-t$ and $x_3=8-t$. All solutions are

$$(x_1,x_2,x_3)=(10-t,t,8-t),\quad t\in\mathbb R.$$

The third equation is automatically satisfied: $(10-t)-(8-t)=2$.
